# FreshCart: English to Uzbek messages and labels

Uzbek, Latin script, polite form. These are the exact texts the app shows. Use them when you quote a message
in Notion acceptance criteria, designs or defect notes. `N` is a number, `X` is a value.

## Days, months and slot labels

Day names (Sun to Sat): `Ya Du Se Ch Pa Ju Sh`. Months (Jan to Dec): `Yan Fev Mar Apr May Iyn Iyl Avg Sen Okt Noy Dek`.
Friday 20 Nov 10:00-12:00 is `Ju 20 Noy`, slot `10:00-12:00`. Thursday 19 Nov is `Pa 19 Noy`.
